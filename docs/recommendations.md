# Recommendations

Five recommendations, each tied to a result in `outputs/insights/findings.json`.

## 1. Pre-position suppression capacity in Ogan Komering Ilir

Evidence: highest mean modelled susceptibility on peat (0.081) and 59 percent of MODIS burned area over 2023 to 2025.
Action: stage pumps, canal blocking crews and aerial assets before the late dry season.
Actors: KLHK Directorate of Fire Control, BNPB, provincial BPBD.
Indicator: time from first detection to suppression.
Priority: high.

## 2. Base warnings on dry air over deep peat after a dry month

Evidence: VPD, peat thickness and previous-month soil moisture are the three strongest drivers, and VPD with peat thickness is the strongest interaction.
Action: raise alerts when VPD exceeds about 0.65 kPa over peat deeper than about 1.5 m after a dry month.
Actors: BMKG, KLHK, BRGM.
Indicator: share of fire-positive cells that were under alert beforehand.
Priority: high.

## 3. Treat Pelalawan differently

Evidence: leave-one-district-out ROC-AUC 0.55, and peat thickness rather than VPD leads its drivers.
Action: add concession, drainage and land-clearing data before relying on a climate-based model there, and prioritise enforcement on ignition.
Actors: KLHK, Riau provincial government.
Indicator: better held-out skill once the new predictors are added.
Priority: medium.

## 4. Watch the intensifying clusters

Evidence: 299 intensifying and 40 new hotspot cells, mostly in northern Kotawaringin Timur and Pelalawan, where fire continued after 2023.
Action: field checks and patrols in those cells in ordinary seasons, not only in El Nino years.
Actors: Manggala Agni, plantation fire units.
Indicator: fire pixel counts in those cells in the next season.
Priority: medium.

## 5. Coordinate haze alerts when fire peaks under west to north winds

Evidence: 14 of 15 district-seasons show fire-weighted winds between west and north on high-activity days.
Action: share high-activity forecasts with the ASEAN Specialised Meteorological Centre.
Actors: BMKG, ASMC.
Indicator: lead time of regional haze advisories.
Priority: medium. The wind proxy gives direction only; exposure needs a dispersion model.
