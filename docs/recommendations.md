# Recommendations

Six actionable recommendations, ordered by priority.

## 1. Pre position suppression resources in the highest risk district

Triggering insight: Ogan Komering Ilir carries the highest mean fire susceptibility (0.160).
Concrete action: stage water pumps, canal blocking crews, and aerial assets before the late dry season.
Relevant actor: KLHK Directorate of Fire Control, BNPB.
Success indicator: reduced time to suppression on first detections.
Priority: High.

## 2. Trigger early warnings on compound dryness

Triggering insight: the VPD and soil moisture interaction is the strongest driver pair (0.087).
Concrete action: build alerts that fire when dry air and dry soil occur together, not on a single index.
Relevant actor: BMKG, KLHK.
Success indicator: earlier and more precise dry season alerts.
Priority: High.

## 3. Differentiate intervention by district

Triggering insight: Pelalawan is nearly unpredictable from climate alone, suggesting human driven ignition.
Concrete action: apply rewetting and canal blocking where dryness dominates, and law enforcement plus land clearing oversight where ignition is human driven.
Relevant actor: KLHK, provincial governments.
Success indicator: driver aligned budget allocation.
Priority: High.

## 4. Operationalise the susceptibility surface as a watch layer

Triggering insight: the model ranks dangerous cells above chance on unseen years and districts.
Concrete action: refresh predictors weekly during the dry season and publish a risk map.
Relevant actor: BMKG, KLHK.
Success indicator: lead time on hotspot escalation.
Priority: Medium.

## 5. Issue transboundary haze pre alerts on northwesterly wind days

Triggering insight: high activity days show smoke export toward the Malacca corridor.
Concrete action: coordinate alerts when high fire radiative power coincides with northwesterly winds.
Relevant actor: ASEAN Specialised Meteorological Centre, BMKG.
Success indicator: earlier regional haze warnings.
Priority: Medium.

## 6. Replace the peat proxy with the national peat map

Triggering insight: the elevation based peat proxy ranked last and residual autocorrelation remains high (Moran's I = 0.5453).
Concrete action: integrate the official peat depth and water table layers, then retrain.
Relevant actor: KLHK, research partners.
Success indicator: reduced peat misclassification and lower residual autocorrelation.
Priority: Medium.
